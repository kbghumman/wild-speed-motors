# Third-party visual assets

The manufacturer logos in `public/brand-logos/` are used only to identify the corresponding vehicle manufacturers in the Wild Speed Motors inventory interface.

Source collection:
- https://github.com/diegojasso/car-logos-SVG
- The source collection states that its SVGs were sourced from Wikimedia Commons under their respective licenses.
